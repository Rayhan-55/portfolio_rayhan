About

I am a final-year Computer Science & Engineering student and AI practitioner with over a year of hands-on project experience in machine learning, LLM applications, and production retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines. My work spans medical-image classification, computer-vision detection and tracking, and public-health and disaster NLP, with an emphasis on explainable, well-calibrated, and reproducible models. I am co-author on several peer-reviewed manuscripts (four as first author) currently under review or in preparation, and I enjoy owning the full path from data cleaning and feature engineering through model selection to a deployed FastAPI service.
